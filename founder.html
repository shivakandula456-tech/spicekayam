Meet Suchitra Anikhindi, founder and owner of KAYAM SPICES.

Founder & Owner

Suchitra Anikhindi

“Three generations. One timeless taste.”

The story of KAYAM SPICES began long before the brand itself, in the kitchens where family knowledge and the familiar flavours of home were shared from grandmother to mother, and from mother to Suchitra.

Her family heritage brings together Telugu and Marathi languages, and the food traditions of South India and Maharashtra. These are more than regional influences; they are part of the memories, practices and sense of belonging carried through the family kitchen.

Today, Suchitra carries that inheritance forward through KAYAM SPICES. The brand brings familiar spice powders, traditional masalas, premixes and podis into one considered pantry, connecting the values of family tradition with the needs of today's kitchens.
